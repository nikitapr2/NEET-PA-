🔵 Section 1: Historical Perspective of Ayurveda

📖 Definition of Ayurveda

📝 ORIGINAL SHLOKA (Charaka Samhita)

"Hitahitam sukham duhkhayustasya hitahitam,
maanam cha taccha yatroktam Āyurvedah sa uchyate"

That which tells us about good life (hita), bad life (ahita), happy life (sukha), unhappy life (duhkha), and their measures — that is Ayurveda.

🧠 MEMORY TRICK

AYURVEDA = ĀYU + VEDA
Āyu = Life | Veda = Knowledge/Science
→ "Science of Life"

🔢 Āyu (Life) = 4 Components

📝 RATTA TRICK: "SISA"

Sharira (Body) + Indriya (Sense Organs) + Sattva (Mind) + Atma (Soul) = ĀYU

📛 4 Synonyms of Āyu

📝 RATTA TRICK: "DJNA"

Dhāri + Jīvita + Nityaga + Anubandha

Dhāri = sustainer | Jīvita = living | Nityaga = ever-moving | Anubandha = continuity

🎯 Quick Fire Facts

  • Ayurveda is an Upaveda of Atharvaveda
  • Dhanvantari = God of Ayurveda (deity)
  • AshtangaSangraha also by Vagbhata (not in BrihatTrayi)

  • Charaka's teacher = Punarvasu Atreya
  • Sushruta's teacher = Dhanvantari

🟡 Section 2: Fundamental Principles

🔢 Sankhya Philosophy — 24 Tattvas

📝 FORMULA: 1+1+1+5+5+5+5+1 = 24

1 Prakriti (Primordial Matter)
1 Mahat / Buddhi (Intellect)
1 Ahankara (Ego)
5 Tanmatras (Subtle elements: Shabda, Sparsha, Rupa, Rasa, Gandha)
5 Panchamahabhutas (Gross elements)
5 Jnanendriyas (Sense organs: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin)
5 Karmendriyas (Action organs: hands, feet, speech, genitals, anus)
1 Manas (Mind)
= 24 Tattvas

🧠 TRICK: "PMATAJTJKM"

Prakriti→Mahat→Ahankara→Tanmatra(5)→Jnaneindriya(5)→Karmendriya(5)→Manas
Count: 3 singles + 5+5+5+5 + 0 Manas = wait...
Easy way: 3 + (4×5) + 1 = 24

🟢 Section 3: Health, Lifestyle & Diet

🏥 Definition of Health (WHO + Ayurveda)

📝 AYURVEDIC DEFINITION OF HEALTH (Sushruta Samhita)

"Sama dosha sama agnischa sama dhatu malakriyaah,
Prasanna atmendriya manah swastha ityabhidhiyate"

= Balanced Doshas + Balanced Agni + Balanced Dhatus + Proper elimination of Malas + Happy Soul + Happy Sense organs + Happy Mind = HEALTH

🧠 TRICK: "DADAM + 3 Happy"

Dosha balanced + Agni balanced + Dhatu balanced + Amal(Mala) normal + Mind/Soul/Senses happy

🍎 Pathyapathya (Wholesome & Unwholesome)

  • Pathya = Wholesome diet/regimen (beneficial)
  • Apathya = Unwholesome diet/regimen (harmful)
  • Satmya = Habitual/adapted diet (body gets used to)

🧘 Sadvritta (Good Conduct/Ethics)

  • Following ethical social conduct for mental health
  • Respecting elders, teachers, gods
  • Non-violence, truth, cleanliness
  • Control of senses = Indriya nigraha

💊 Rasayana (Rejuvenation Therapy)

  • Rasayana = anti-aging, immunity boost, longevity therapy
  • Example: Chyawanprash (most famous rasayana)
  • Promotes Ojas (vital essence, immunity)
  • Vajikarana = aphrodisiac therapy (reproductive health)

🔴 Section 4: Disease, Diagnosis & Treatment

🔍 Nidana Panchaka (5 factors of Disease)

📝 RATTA: "NPSUP" = Nidana-Purvarupa-Rupa-Samprapti-Upashaya

1. Nidana = Causative factors (etiology)
2. Purvarupa = Premonitory symptoms (before disease fully appears)
3. Rupa = Actual signs & symptoms
4. Samprapti = Pathogenesis (how disease develops)
5. Upashaya = Therapeutic test (what relieves it)

🧠 TRICK: "Nana Pakhi Rani Sundar Udti"

Nidana → Purvarupa → Rupa → Samprapti → Upashaya
